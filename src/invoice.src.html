How privacy, OCR and duplicate checks workFiles are read inside your browser and are not uploaded anywhere. Scanned PDFs and photos are read with OCR: the first time, a small text-recognition engine is downloaded (internet needed), your file itself never leaves this device. OCR is less accurate than text PDFs, so those invoices are always marked Needs Review. A small fingerprint of each processed invoice (supplier, number, date, total) is kept in this browser to spot duplicates in later batches.
